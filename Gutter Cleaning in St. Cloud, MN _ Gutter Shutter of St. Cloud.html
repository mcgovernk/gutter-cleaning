The DIY Reality

Why DIY Gutter Cleaning Is a Bad Deal

It looks like a quick Saturday job. It rarely is.

Photo: homeowner on a ladderor clogged gutter close-up
🪜

Ladder & roof risk

Wet leaves, uneven ground, and steep rooflines make falls a real danger. It’s not worth an ER visit.

🤢

Gross, slimy muck

Rotting sludge, mosquito larvae, and the occasional dead critter. Gloves only go so far.

🚿

Hidden downspout clogs

Scooping the top is easy. Clogs deeper in the downspout keep backing up and overflowing.

🔧

Damaged gutters

Scrapers, hoses, and shifting ladders bend gutters, loosen hangers, and open up leaky seams.

⏳

Time & gear

Ladder, stabilizer, gloves, bags, hose. That’s a whole weekend, twice a year, every year.

👀

Missed warning signs

Untrained eyes miss rotting fascia, sagging runs, and failing seams until water gets inside.

💸

Costly water damage

A half-done job still lets water reach your siding, foundation, and landscaping.

FAQ

Quick Answers

 

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

It depends on your home’s size and gutter condition. Your inspection and estimate are free, and you’ll know the price before any work starts.

Do I need to be home?

Not for cleaning. We’ll confirm your appointment window and send a before-and-after photo report when we’re done. For estimates, we’ll want a few minutes with you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards and checks, and offer easy financing on new gutter installations. Ask at your appointment.

Will my gutters still overflow in heavy rain?

Clean gutters drain far better. If yours overflow even when clean, our clog-free system is built larger than standard 5-inch gutters and backed by a lifetime no-clog warranty.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most Minnesota homes need it at least twice a year, usually spring and fall. Homes near trees may need more. We’ll tell you what yours needs.
